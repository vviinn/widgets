# Content Slots 

You can use [Slots](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Web_Components/Using_templates_and_slots#adding_flexibility_with_slots) to change and customize the contents of the following `Vviinn Widgets`:

- [Discovery Slider](#slider)
- [Discovery Set](#set)
- [Search Modal](#search-modal)
- [Filters](#filters)
- [Product List](#product-list)

## Discovery

Here are slots that could be used for [Discovery Slider](?path=/docs/widgets-discovery-slider--docs), [Discovery Set](?path=/docs/widgets-discovery-set--docs) and [One-click Discovery](?path=/docs/widgets-one-click-discovery--docs) widgets.

### Discovery Slider Slots:

* vviinn-recommendations-title - *Recommendations title text*
* vviinn-basket-button-text - *Text of Add to Basket button*
* vviinn-energy-label-data-sheet-text - *Text of the data sheet link for the energy label*
* vviinn-basket-button-text - *Text of Add to Basket button*
* vviinn-basket-button-icon - *Icon of Add to Basket button*
* vviinn-basket-button-text-pressed - *Text of pressed Add to Basket button*
* vviinn-basket-button-icon-pressed - *Icon of pressed Add to Basket button*
* vviinn-favorite-icon - *Icon of Favorite button*
* vviinn-favorite-icon-pressed - *Icon of pressed Favorite button*

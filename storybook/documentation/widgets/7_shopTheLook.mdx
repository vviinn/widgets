# Shop The Look

The `Shop The Look` widget enables interactive visual product discovery directly on images. Users can explore hotspots on fashion, furniture, DIY, and lifestyle images to discover and purchase featured products, creating an engaging shopping experience that bridges inspiration and commerce.

## Key Features

- **Interactive Hotspots**: Clickable areas on images reveal product information
- **Visual Search**: Powered by AI to identify products within images
- **Multiple Images**: Support for single or multiple images across various categories
- **Responsive Design**: Adapts to different screen sizes and orientations
- **Widget Styles**: Choose between rounded and cornered visual styles
- **Cropper Mode**: Enable manual area selection for precise product targeting
- **Upload Mode**: Allow users to upload their own images for instant product discovery
